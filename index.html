Five core modules make up the experience — each one demonstrated in the flow above.

₹

Fare estimation

Live pricing based on distance and vehicle tier, shown before the rider commits to a booking.

◎

Live driver tracking

An animated route shows the driver's position, remaining distance and ETA in real time.

✓

Digital payments

UPI, card or wallet — a clear fare breakdown before the rider pays.

★

Two-way ratings

Riders rate drivers after every trip, keeping quality visible across the network.

≡

Ride history

Every past trip logged with route, fare and the rating given — searchable at a glance.

◈

Multiple ride tiers

Economy, Comfort and Pool options let riders trade off price against speed and privacy.

How a ride flows through the system

01

Request

Rider enters pickup and drop points; the system estimates fare and ETA.

02

Match

The nearest available driver is assigned and shares live location.

03

Trip

Rider tracks the car on the map until pickup, then through to the destination.

04

Close-out

Payment is settled digitally, and both parties rate the trip.
